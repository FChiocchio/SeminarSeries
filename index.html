About the series

A weekly forum for new economics

The ESCP Economics Seminar Series is the department's standing forum for presenting and discussing cutting-edge research. We host external speakers alongside ESCP faculty, with talks spanning theory, empirics and policy. Seminars are open to faculty, PhD students and master's students across all ESCP campuses.

Sessions run on most Wednesdays during term. Talks are typically 75 minutes including discussion, followed by informal exchanges with the speaker.
